## Scope and method

D1 is a decision session that wrote documents, not code, so PRD section 13 and the conventions checklist do not apply. The table below takes the brief's "Done when" and "Watch for" items and this review's five checks in their place. D1 had already run four verification subagents, so this review did not re-extract the 86 PRDs. Instead it ran targeted checks:

- **Deliverables.** I read the brief, the D1 section of `.claude/skills/decide-session/SKILL.md`, the handoff, and the structure, index and section 4 of every deliverable myself.

- **Spot checks.** Three fresh subagents worked from the PRD text:
  1. **Inventory:** 52 rows of INVENTORY.md and 8 of INVENTORY-VALUES-AND-CALLS.md, at least 4 from every folder and 3 from CONVENTIONS. A completeness grep covered 14 elements.
  2. **Conflicts:** 33 CF entries, the section 4 table and 14 AU entries, citing every folder plus CONVENTIONS and the README. About 380 cited lines were opened.
  3. **Watch for:** the brief's list, checked by grep over all 86 PRDs.
- **Re-verification.** I re-checked every finding below against the PRD line myself before recording it. I dropped one subagent claim: it said `url_hash` "does not appear in CONFLICTS.md". The hits are `canonical_url_hash`, but the bare `url_hash` field is indeed absent, so the claim survives only as note 10. I also dropped one citation I could not confirm (an fb-client-webhook-receiver §5.2 L59 demotion).
